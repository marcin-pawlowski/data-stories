<div class="section">
  <h2 class="section-title">What this is</h2>
  <div class="prose">

My day job is building AI agents for analytics teams — the repetitive parts no one
wants to do by hand: tracking audits, anomaly detection, consent checks. This site is
the same machinery pointed somewhere more interesting: public data.

A discovery agent scans a few dozen sources, picks what looks worth attention that
week, checks whether the data is actually good enough to chart, and drafts the story.
Then I edit it, build the visuals, and publish. Built with Claude Code, the way I build
everything else.

No black boxes. Every story says where the data came from and what it doesn't tell you.
